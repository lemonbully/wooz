OneWooz - Kaam ki Report
Date: 24 September 2026

1. Newsletter (footer wala "Subscribe") ka issue

1.1 Pehle store ke footer me jo newsletter subscribe box hai, usme email daal ke
    Subscribe dabate the to error aa jaata tha aur email save nahi hota tha.

1.2 Ab wo poori tarah theek kar diya hai. Customer email daal ke subscribe karega
    to seedha save ho jaayega, koi error nahi aayega.

1.3 Ye fix store ke dono type ke pages par laga diya hai, normal store bhi aur
    collaboration (UGC) wala store bhi.


2. Subscribers dashboard me dikhna + Excel export + notification

2.1 Pehle jo log newsletter subscribe karte the, wo owner ke dashboard ke
    Subscribers page par dikhte hi nahi the. Ab har subscriber wahan dikhega.

2.2 Subscribers page par ek naya button laga diya hai "Export to Excel". Ek click
    me saari subscriber emails ki Excel file download ho jaayegi.

2.3 Search karke bhi export kar sakte hain, matlab agar aap kisi khaas email ko
    filter karte hain to sirf wahi export hongi.

2.4 Jab bhi koi naya customer subscribe karega, owner ke dashboard ki ghanti
    (notification bell) me uski jaankari aa jaayegi aur activity list me bhi
    dikhega.


3. Collaboration store ka product search

3.1 Collaboration user ke store me upar jo "Search products" box hai, usme ab
    likhte hi turant products filter hone lagte hain.

3.2 Alag se koi search button dabane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Jaise jaise type
    karoge, waise waise result aate jaayenge.


4. Customer Login page (collaboration store)

4.1 Pehle jab customer teen dot wale menu se "Customer Login" par jaata tha to
    page bilkul khaali (blank) dikhta tha. Ye theek kar diya hai.

4.2 Ab login page aur login ke baad ka account page dono theek se, sundar aur
    mobile jaisa card layout me dikhte hain.

4.3 Account page par customer ke orders, store credits aur wishlist saaf saaf
    dikhte hain. Orders ko tap karke uski poori detail bhi dekh sakte hain.


5. Ek zaroori baat - data same rahega

5.1 Agar customer same Gmail se login karta hai, to uski wishlist aur orders
    dono jagah same dikhenge. Matlab collaboration store aur asli (owner) website
    store dono par ek jaisa data.

5.2 Agar wishlist collaboration store se add ki, to wo owner store par bhi dikhegi
    aur ulta bhi. Kyunki dono ka data ek hi jagah judaa hua hai.


6. Poora store ek mobile card ke andar

6.1 Desktop par collaboration store beech me ek mobile phone jaise card ke andar
    khulta hai. Ab store ka har page usi card ke andar hi dikhta hai.

6.2 Cart, checkout, account, order confirmation, sab card ke andar hi rehte hain,
    kahin bahar nahi jaate.


7. Contact, About, Policy, Core pages, Blogs (collaboration store)

7.1 Ab collaboration store ka Contact page bilkul waisa hi content dikhata hai
    jaisa owner ki asli website par dikhta hai. Address, email, phone, office
    time, map aur message form sab aa gaya hai.

7.2 About Us page bhi owner store jaisa hi ban gaya hai, uske saare section aur
    images ke saath.

7.3 Privacy policy aur baaki saari policy pages theek se dikhne lage hain, poore
    style ke saath.

7.4 Blogs ki list aur kisi ek particular blog ka poora content ab sahi se render
    hota hai. Pehle blog ke andar ka content khaali aata tha, ab theek hai.

7.5 Core pages ab tree jaise (page ke andar sub-page, uske andar aur page) waise
    hi dikhte hain jaise owner website me. "Contents" menu se aaram se navigate
    kar sakte hain.

7.6 Jo bhi content editor (TinyMCE) me likha jaata hai, wo ab store par bilkul
    waise hi sundar dikhta hai, headings, list, table, image sab ke saath.


8. Checkout page ki spacing

8.1 Checkout page par left aur right me halki si jagah (spacing) de di hai taaki
    content ekdam kinare se na chipke aur dekhne me saaf lage.


9. Cart ka drawer (side se khulne wala cart)

9.1 Pehle desktop par cart drawer card ke andar theek se fit nahi ho raha tha,
    thoda bahar nikal jaata tha aur text kat jaata tha. Ye theek kar diya hai.

9.2 Ab cart drawer poora card ke andar hi, sahi jagah par khulta hai.


10. Cart button har page par

10.1 Pehle upar navbar me jo cart icon tha, wo dabane par home page par le jaata
     tha, jo galat lagta tha. Wo upar wala cart icon hata diya hai.

10.2 Ab neeche right side me jo kaala gol floating cart button hai, wo store ke
     har page par rehta hai. Use dabate hi cart wahin side se khul jaata hai,
     kahin jaana nahi padta.


11. Pull to refresh (neeche se kheech kar refresh)

11.1 Mobile par page ko upar se kheech kar refresh karne ka feature chal raha hai.

11.2 Pehle "Refreshing" ke time ek ghoomta hua loader dikhta tha. Aap ki
     request ke hisaab se wo loader hata diya hai. Ab sirf saaf text dikhta hai.


12. Product page ke "View Details" tabs (collaboration store)

12.1 Product page par "View Details" kholne par jo tabs aate hain (Description,
     Item Details, Manufacturer & Packer, Others), unme se Item Details tab me
     saari details nahi dikhti thi.

12.2 Ab Item Details tab me product ki poori jaankari aati hai, jaise HSN code,
     GST, material, colour, use, handling time, accessories, product aur package
     ke dimensions, weight, country of origin, return type, warranty aur baaki
     specifications.

12.3 "Others" tab me bhi ab extra details dikhne lagi hain jo pehle khaali aati
     thi.

12.4 Matlab ab har tab ke andar uski poori aur sahi detail dikhti hai, bilkul
     owner store jaisa.


13. Collaboration store me cart update ka error (plus/minus/remove)

13.1 Pehle collaboration (UGC) store me cart ke andar quantity plus/minus karne par
     ya koi item remove karne par error aa jaata tha (CSRF Forbidden - 403). Item
     na to badalta tha na hatta tha.

13.2 Iska kaaran ye tha ki cart ka security token (CSRF token) galat jagah se dhoonda
     ja raha tha, isliye khaali chala jaata tha aur request reject ho jaati thi.

13.3 Ab ye poori tarah theek kar diya hai. Cart me plus, minus aur remove sab sahi
     se, bina kisi error ke kaam karte hain.

13.4 Ye fix cart ke side drawer aur product page dono jagah laga diya hai.


14. Collaboration user ke dashboard ki poori nayi look (redesign)

14.1 Collaboration (UGC) user jab apne dashboard par jaata tha to wo dekhne me
     accha nahi lag raha tha. Ab poore dashboard ki UI badal di hai, ekdam
     saaf, structured aur sophisticated, OneWooz ke apne design style me.

14.2 Ab sab kuch square aur clean hai (gol kone hata diye), halki hairline
     lines se har section alag dikhta hai, aur ek hi accent colour (purple)
     jagah jagah use hua hai. Fonts bhi premium aur professional lagte hain.

14.3 Upar ek sticky bar (naam + sign out), phir badे saaf stat cards (Orders,
     Earned, Paid out, Outstanding), aur uske neeche numbered sections:
     "01 Your orders", "02 Payouts", "03 Customize your storefront".

14.4 Poora dashboard mobile, tablet aur desktop, teeno par sahi se fit hota hai.
     Table mobile par side me scroll ho jaati hai, cards apne aap ek ya do
     column me set ho jaate hain.

14.5 Kaam pehle jaisa hi chalta hai, bas dikhne me bahut behtar ho gaya hai.
     Orders, payout request, branding, reels, product photos, sab waise hi
     working hain. Login page bhi isi naye style me match kar diya hai.


15. Product video ka icon aur full-screen video (collaboration store)

15.1 Collaboration store ke product page par, product ki image ke neeche right
     side me ek chhota "Video" icon aa jaata hai, lekin sirf tab jab us product
     ka video ho.

15.2 Us icon par click karte hi video poori screen me khul jaata hai aur apne
     aap chalne (autoplay) lag jaata hai, aawaz ke saath.

15.3 Upar right side me ek cross (X) button hai. Use dabate hi ya Esc key se
     video band ho jaata hai aur ruk jaata hai.

15.4 Ye uploaded video file aur YouTube link, dono ke liye kaam karta hai aur
     har device par sahi se render hota hai.


16. Email template banane wale AI chatbot me "Design style" chunne ki suvidha

16.1 Admin panel me jo AI chatbot se email template banate hain, wahan ab upar ek
     naya option aa gaya hai "Design style". Ismein 19 tarah ke ready design
     style hain, jaise Minimalism, Maximalism, Futuristic, Vector art, Collage
     art, Retro, Cyberpunk, Pop art, Glass morphism, Clay, Pixel art, Editorial,
     Y2K, Swiss, Surreal, Bohemian, Victorian, Graffiti aur Aurora.

16.2 Ab jo bhi style choose karenge, wo poora look (colour, font, layout, design
     ka andaaz) apne aap us email template par lag jaayega. Baar baar likh kar
     samjhane ki zaroorat nahi ki "mujhe aisa design chahiye".

16.3 Matlab chhota sa ek line ka brief likho aur style choose kar lo, template
     seedha usi style me, saaf aur professional ban kar aa jaayega.

16.4 "Auto" option bhi hai, jisme AI khud sahi look chun leta hai (pehle jaisa).
     Chuna hua style yaad bhi rehta hai, aur har generation usi style me banti hai.

16.5 Saare design email me sahi se chalne wale (Gmail, Outlook waghairah) tareeke
     se banaye gaye hain, taaki template har jagah theek dikhe.
