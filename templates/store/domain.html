DNS setup

At your domain registrar / DNS provider, add this record. It usually takes a few minutes to an hour to take effect.

Type
CNAME
Name / Host

TTL

Using your root domain (no subdomain)?

Most providers don’t allow a CNAME on the root (yourbrand.com). Use an ALIAS / ANAME record to {{ cname_target }} if your provider supports it, or point the www subdomain with the CNAME above and redirect the root to it.

After the record is live, click Verify now above. Once verified, your storefront answers on your domain; secure HTTPS is provisioned automatically shortly after.
