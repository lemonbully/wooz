How totals add up

Every row: paid amount + CGST + SGST + IGST + others = total amount paid. CGST + SGST when the user's state matches OneWooz's billing state, IGST otherwise. Subscriptions follow the owner's bill; commerce fee bills use the GST printed on each bill. A Summary sheet totals everything by product and by state.
