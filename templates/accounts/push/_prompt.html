One-time setup

Get order alerts on your iPhone

Apple only allows alerts from websites added to your Home Screen. It takes three taps:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open OneWooz from your Home Screen and tap Turn on alerts.
