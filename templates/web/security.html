OneWooz / Security

Your customers trust you.
We protect what that means.

When someone buys from your store, they hand over their name, address and payment. Here is what we do to keep that safe — in plain words, and what it means for you.

{# ------------------------------------------------------- How we protect #}
01How we protect you

Six protections, working quietly in the background.

Only you get into your account
Signing in is hard to fake and easy for you. You can add a second step, or sign in with your own device instead of a password — so there's nothing to steal or guess.
Your store stands alone
One merchant can never see another's orders or customers. That separation is part of the foundation — not a rule someone has to remember to follow.
Sensitive details are locked away
The things that matter most — payment connections and private records — are stored scrambled. If they were ever copied, they'd be useless to whoever took them.
Everything travels encrypted
Every page — on our address or your own domain — is served over a secure connection, so what your buyers type can't be read in transit. We keep it valid for you, always.
Links give nothing away
Addresses and references can't be guessed or counted through. Nobody can tinker with a link to reach an order or an account that isn't theirs.
You can see what happens
Every sign-in and every sensitive change is recorded and visible to you — including the attempts that failed. If anything looks off, you'll know first.
{# ------------------------------------------------------------- Promises #}
02What we promise

Security is also about what we won't do.

01
We never sell or rent your data
Your customers are yours. We don't hand them, or their details, to anyone.
02
We keep only what's needed
We hold the information required to run your store — not a hoard of extra data “just in case”.
03
You can take it all and leave
Export your data and point your domain elsewhere whenever you like. Nothing is held hostage.
04
If something goes wrong, we tell you
No quiet cover-ups. If an incident touches your store, you hear it from us, promptly and plainly.
