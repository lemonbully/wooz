{% comment %} Email marketing, as the four steps an owner actually takes (home + platform): a single ink rule with a node per step, each a mono index, a short title and one line of detail. Typographic and structured, no drawn mockups. Styles: .ow-emflow in onewooz-pal.css. {% endcomment %}
  1. 01

    Choose the audience

    All your customers, or lists you build and import. Anyone who unsubscribed is left out automatically.

  2. 02

    Compose the email

    Start from a designed template, or describe it and let AI write it. Your logo, products and a shop button fill in.

  3. 03

    Send or automate

    Send now, schedule it, or set a welcome series that runs day by day. Every email goes out in your store’s name.

  4. 04

    Measure what worked

    Opens and clicks for every email and series, so you know which message brought buyers back.
