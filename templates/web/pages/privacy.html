We hold two kinds of information: yours, as a OneWooz customer, and your shoppers', as data you are responsible for.

Your account

Your name, email address, phone number if you give one, and a record of when and from where you signed in. We use it to run your account, secure it, and contact you about your stores. We do not sell it and we do not share it for advertising.

Your shoppers' data

Orders, addresses and contact details entered on your storefront belong to you. We store and process them so your store works — taking payments, printing labels, sending order emails. We do not use them for our own purposes, and we do not contact your customers except on your instruction.

Third parties

Payments are handled by your chosen gateway and shipping by your chosen courier; each receives only what it needs to complete the job. We store the credentials you give us for those services encrypted, and you can revoke them at any time.

Deletion

Delete a store and its content goes with it. Close your account and we remove your personal data, keeping only what we are legally required to retain — chiefly invoices and the audit record of actions taken on the account.

Contact

Questions about any of this: privacy@onewooz.com.
