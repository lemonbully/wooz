A good room is not about owning more furniture. It is about a few pieces that work so well together that the room feels finished, calm and easy to live in.

Start with the room

Measure first, then decide how the room will be used. A reading corner, a dining nook and a family sofa each ask for different proportions.

“Buy fewer pieces, but buy the ones you will keep.”

The five pieces

  • A sofa deep enough to nap on
  • A solid wood table that seats everyone
  • One lounge chair with a lamp beside it
  • Open shelving for books and ceramics
  • A rug that grounds the room

Mix materials

Pair warm wood with linen and one metal finish. Repeat each material at least twice so the room feels considered rather than collected.

Buy slowly

Live with the room for a month before adding more. The gaps usually tell you what is missing.
