A smart home is not about owning more gadgets. It is about a few devices that work so well together that you forget they are there.

Start with one room

Pick the room you spend the most time in and automate one routine there, usually lighting in the evening.

“The best smart home is the one you never have to think about.”

The five devices

  • A tunable floor lamp for evenings
  • A pendant over the table or desk
  • Headphones for calls and focus
  • A diffuser on a sleep schedule
  • A watch that tracks your rest

Make scenes

Group devices into scenes like Wake, Focus and Wind down, then put them on a schedule. One tap, or none at all.

Keep it simple

Live with one room for a few weeks before adding more. The routines you miss tell you what to automate next.
