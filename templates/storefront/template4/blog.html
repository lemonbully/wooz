A good routine is not about owning more products. It is about a few formulas that work so well together that looking after your skin takes five minutes, and still feels like a ritual.

Start with a palette

Pick two neutrals and one accent. We like cream and indigo with a warm rust. Everything you add should sit comfortably next to at least three other pieces.

“The best capsule is the one you forget you planned. It just works.”

The twelve pieces

  • Three heavyweight tees: white, black, one colour
  • One oxford shirt and one denim shirt
  • Straight fit jeans and a relaxed trouser
  • A knit layer and a light jacket
  • White sneakers and one statement pair
  • A bag that carries a laptop

Mix, match, repeat

Lay everything on the bed and build outfits until you run out of ideas. Most people find twenty or more combinations. Photograph them and keep the album for busy mornings.

Caring for it all

Wash cold, dry in shade and fold knits instead of hanging them. Good care doubles the life of a garment, which is the most sustainable thing you can do.
