A good routine is not about owning more products. It is about a few formulas that work so well together that looking after your skin takes five minutes, and still feels like a ritual.

Know your skin

Dry, oily, combination or sensitive: your skin type decides textures, not steps. Gel for oily skin, cream for dry, and fewer actives if your skin stings easily.

“The best routine is the one you actually do every day.”

The five steps

  • A gentle cleanser, one pump is enough
  • A hydrating toner or essence
  • One targeted serum, vitamin C or niacinamide
  • A moisturiser that suits your skin type
  • SPF 50, every single morning

Layer it right

Go from thinnest to thickest texture and give each layer thirty seconds to sink in. Serums before creams, and sunscreen always last.

Stay consistent

Give any new product four weeks before you judge it. Skin renews roughly every 28 days, so patience is the most underrated ingredient.
