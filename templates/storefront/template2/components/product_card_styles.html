{% comment %}Styles for components/product_card.html and the grids that hold it. Every product grid in this theme (homepage Best Sellers, category pages, All products, the product page's "You may also like" strip) includes this once, after the page's own styles, so the card looks the same everywhere. Spacing is fluid: the gap between cards (.ow-pgrid) and each card's margin scale with the viewport through clamp(). A narrower screen gives up gutter, not card, so the card keeps the proportions it has on a wide screen instead of shrinking while the gaps stay wide.{% endcomment %}
