    Your privacy matters to {{ store }}. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our store or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have.

    Information we collect

    • Order details: your name, email address, phone number, and billing and delivery addresses.
    • Payment details: payments are handled by secure, certified payment providers. We do not store your full card or bank details.
    • Usage information: pages viewed, device and browser type, collected to keep the store fast and secure.

    How we use your information

    • To process, ship and deliver your orders, and to keep you updated about them.
    • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
    • To send offers or newsletters, only when you have chosen to receive them.
    • To prevent fraud and keep our store secure.

    Sharing your information

    We never sell your personal information. We share it only with partners who help us run the store, such as payment providers and courier companies, and only as much as they need to do their job, or when the law requires it.

    Cookies

    We use cookies to keep your cart and sign-in working and to understand how our store is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

    Data security

    We protect your information with industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections. No online service can be completely secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

    Your choices

    You can view and update your details from your account page, unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time, and close your account whenever you like. We keep a record of past orders where the law requires it.

    Contact us

    Questions about this policy? {% if website.support_email %}Write to us at {{ website.support_email }}.{% else %}Please reach out through our contact page.{% endif %}

    {% elif policy_key == "refund" %}

    We want you to love what you ordered from {{ store }}. If something is not right, here is how cancellations, returns and refunds work.

    Cancelling an order

    You can cancel an order before it ships from your account page, or by contacting us with your order number. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but you may be able to return it.

    Returns

    • Items should be unused, in their original condition and packaging, with all tags attached.
    • Some items, such as personal-care or hygiene products, may not be returnable. This is noted on the product page where it applies.
    • To start a return, contact us with your order number and the reason for the return.

    Damaged or incorrect items

    If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible after delivery with your order number and photos. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund.

    Refunds

    Once your return is received and checked, we will let you know and process your refund to the original payment method. For cash on delivery orders, we will refund to a bank account or UPI ID you share with us. Banks may take a few business days to show the amount.
